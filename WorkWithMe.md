# Work with me

With AI automatically expanding the amount of information and carrying us across domains in seconds, I think the most relevant aspects of human specialization are the universal, transferable ones. Mathematics stands out here. It trains us to establish a few stable points of understanding and use them as lighthouses while searching for a simple path through the vast landscape of information, at whose end is fundamental human understanding, mastery, and ownership together with the ability to communicate what we have learned along the way clearly to our human communities.

On the one hand, I am trained to work with pen and paper and write technical text or code line by line, from low-level details to high-level abstractions. On the other hand, I can “vibe” freely or iterate on a human-owned declarative document and then harness AI agents to materialize the design autonomously. I have a track record of starting and completing independent academic work, software projects, and research software projects. I’ve also mentored and supported others.

**Mathematization.** I enjoy digging into unfamiliar fields where the ideas are still sketchy, choosing the right definitions, formulating statements, and proving and formalizing results. The work starts with an idea and ends with a theory.

**De-LLMification.** I can sort through AI-generated artifacts and transform raw information into a piece of work that can be owned and transferred to another human.

**Computification.** My training at Wolfram taught me to look for ways to make things computable. I can design a language for a problem or area with natural parameters and an effective, modular API. I take into account accessibility for people and AI agents so that the result can be used to scaffold experiments, test hypotheses in bounded cases, or support technical writing with computational checks.

**Mentorship.** I enjoy supporting people in doing research they’re happy with and sharing what they find. I can help formulate a research question, scope a research area, define the output, and give ongoing feedback. I ask questions, pedantically push for details, and help keep the work on track. I can also help get a project unstuck and find a way forward when it seems like a lost cause.

**Scientific Communication.** I can help with scientific outreach, including contacting the right people and presenting results in a paper, repository, or interactive website. I can also set up and advertise livestreams or help make animations and music with AI.
